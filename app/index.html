How it works

Four moves. That's the whole game.

You never wait for a turn. Play a few rolls whenever you like, and your Chog keeps earning while you're away.

1

Open an account

Deposit $CHOG into your Chog. That's its cash for the city.

2

Roll

Your Chog moves. Your buying plan buys Bricks (shares) of the street it lands on.

3

Collect rent

Every Chog that lands on your street pays rent to its Brick holders. Claim it from your Desk.

4

Stay or sell

Bricks never leave the Chog. Cashing out costs 10 to 20%. Selling the whole Chog keeps everything.

The board

Six squares are real Monad apps.

Land on one and your Chog does the real thing on Monad, without leaving the game. The positions belong to the NFT.

Why your Chog gets more valuable

The land belongs to the NFT, not to your wallet.

Bricks, stamps and Prestige are recorded against your Chog's token ID. They can't be withdrawn or sent anywhere. Sell the Chog and the buyer gets the whole empire. That's what makes every Chog worth more than its floor.

Nothing is minted

Every coin you earn was paid by another Chog or a fan. The game never prints money.

Leaving costs, staying pays

Cash-outs are taxed 10 to 20% and the tax goes to the players who stay. Every day you stay adds 1% Prestige.

Fair for newcomers

One Chog can own at most 40% of a street, and the cheapest streets are reserved for small Chogs.

Same board. Pick your stakes.
